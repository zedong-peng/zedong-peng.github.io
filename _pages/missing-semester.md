MIT's [The Missing Semester of Your CS Education](https://missing.csail.mit.edu/) teaches the tools that classes skip and work uses every day: the shell, Git, editors. I watched it before AI. My undergraduate program never taught these tools, and I learned how much they mattered during an internship at Intel.

Research now has its own missing semester. The tools for reading papers, writing them and running a project with other people have changed, and school has not caught up. Each post in this series covers one tool: what it replaces, what replaces it, and how to set it up.
