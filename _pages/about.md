**Research:** My research focuses on building and applying large language models to solve meaningful problems across diverse domains.


Recently, I was inspired by Karpathy's [LLM Wiki](https://github.com/karpathy/llm-wiki) and personalized it to build my own [Research Wiki](https://github.com/zedong-peng/llmwiki), which has replaced Zotero in my workflow for now. 
Karpathy also mentioned parametric memory, a concept that Mingzhe discussed with me about a year ago. I attempted to implement it but failed [[blog post]](/blog/2026/failure-of-parametric-memory/). 


**Previously:** I was a research intern at the [NCTI for EDA](https://www.nctieda.com), where I worked with [Prof. Qiang Xu](https://cure-lab.github.io/author/qiang-xu-徐强/) and had a fantastic year with [CURELab](https://cure-lab.github.io/) on LLM4EDA, contributing to HLS-related work [[blog post]](/blog/2026/forgehls-and-diffhls/). I also worked as a research intern at [Intel](https://www.intel.com), collaborating with [Yuan Zhou](https://www.linkedin.com/in/zhouyuan) on [[Gluten]](https://github.com/apache/gluten) and [[vLLM]](https://vllm.ai). I obtained my Bachelor's degree in Electronic Engineering from [Shanghai Jiao Tong University](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn), where I worked with [Prof. Liyao Xiang](http://xiangliyao.cn).
