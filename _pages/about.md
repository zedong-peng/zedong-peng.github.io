**Previously:** I was a research intern at the [NCTI for EDA](https://www.nctieda.com) (2024-2026), where I worked with [Prof. Qiang Xu](https://cure-lab.github.io/author/qiang-xu-徐强/) on [ForgeHLS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03255): building a 400k-design hardware dataset and post-training LLMs on it with a 24-GPU cluster, which raised Llama 3's synthesis pass rate from 19% to 69%. I was also a research intern at [Intel](https://www.intel.com) (2023-2024), where I worked on performance tuning for the Intel CPU backend of [vLLM](https://vllm.ai).

I received my bachelor's degree with honors in Electronic Engineering from [SJTU](https://www.sjtu.edu.cn) in 2024, where I worked with [Prof. Liyao Xiang](http://xiangliyao.cn) on AI security.
